The app can show a **Discord Rich Presence** for what you are watching. It is a normal Discord feature, so it follows Discord's own settings.

- **Incognito mode** pauses Discord presence along with history and scrobbling.
- If presence is not showing, check Discord's own privacy settings and that the app is not in incognito mode.

The app never posts to a channel on its own.
