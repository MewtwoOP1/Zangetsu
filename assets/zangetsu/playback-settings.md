**Settings → Playback** is where the buffer size lives. Raising it helps when playback dips on a weak connection.

In the player you also have:

- **Speed** control.
- **Subtitles** and their styling.
- **Quality**, where the stream offers more than one. The quality button lists the resolutions inside the stream you are playing. If a source only offers one file per quality, those appear as **separate servers** in the sources sheet instead, and a source that reports nothing shows no quality button — that is honest rather than broken.
- **Gestures** for seek, brightness and volume.

Still stalling after raising the buffer? Try another server, then another source, then an external player.
