Yes.

1. Tap the **download icon** on an episode, or use the **batch button** on the episode list to grab several at once.
2. Streams that arrive in pieces are **stitched into a single playable file**, so downloads behave like ordinary videos — including seeking.
3. Downloads live under the **Downloads** section, and you can choose the folder in settings.

Downloads depend on the source supporting them. A source whose stream cannot be fetched as a file will not offer a download.

To confirm a download works offline, put the device in airplane mode and play it from the Downloads section.
