1. Open **Settings → Check for updates**. The app checks GitHub Releases and offers the new version from there.
2. There is an **opt-in beta channel** in settings if you want pre-releases.
3. You can also just install a newer APK over the top. That works and **keeps your data**.

Since v1.5.0 the in-app update **downloads the right APK for your device and verifies it**, which is what fixed the "package appears to be invalid" problem some people hit when the wrong file was picked for them.

The releases page, where the notes for every build are listed, is https://github.com/Spyou/Zangetsu/releases
