Since **v2.3.6** the app has its own **Stremio addon** support, alongside the source ecosystems.

- Add, manage, enable and reorder addons in the app's addon settings.
- Browse **addon catalogues** from Home, with search tabs and filters for type, catalogue and genre.
- Pick a playback link from a grouped list that shows each addon's description, formatting and file size.
- Addon subtitles work, and **resume from Continue Watching** works with addons too.

On Android TV there is a dedicated **Stremio tab on Home** with clearer remote focus.

Addons are third-party services, exactly like extensions: the app does not host, endorse or control them, and they can stop working without notice.
