Yes — movies and TV are part of what the app plays, alongside anime, manga and light novels.

- They are **not bundled**. A movie or show appears only if one of your installed sources carries it, so add a source that covers films before expecting to find one.
- Search results are grouped by source, so it is worth adding more than one source before concluding a title is unavailable.
- Some sources are **movie-only** or **series-only**, so a title missing from one catalogue may be in another ecosystem's sources.
- Movies and TV do not have the same catalogue as anime, and the source switcher only offers sources that fit what you are doing.

If nothing at all carries a title, that is a catalogue gap in the sources you have, not an app problem.
