Different sites number seasons and episodes differently, and the app shows what the source you opened the title from says.

- Some sites use **absolute numbering** (episode 14 continues the first season's count), others restart each season. The same title can look different on two sources.
- Some series are **split into cours** part-way through a season, and a site may list a cour as its own season.
- If the numbering looks wrong, **open the title from another source** and compare.
- **Auto Resolve** is what matches the catalogue entry to the right stream, so it is worth re-running from the title's page when the match looks wrong.

A mismatch between the episode number in the list and the number on the stream itself is the source's mapping, and the source's author is who can fix it.
