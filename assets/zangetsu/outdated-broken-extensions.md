Extensions are written against sites that change. When a site changes, its extension usually gets fixed **within days**.

1. Open **Settings → Sources** and look for the **update badge**.
2. Update the extension. Reinstalling it from its repository also picks up a newer version.
3. If it will not update, remove it and install it again from its repository.
4. Run **Settings → Sources → Source health** to confirm whether it still works at all.
5. Try another extension for the same title, to tell a dead extension from a dead site.

A removed or renamed repository can also make an installed extension look broken — the app cannot reach a URL that no longer exists.

Do not expect any third-party extension to work for ever. They are community-maintained, they are not endorsed by the app project, and they can disappear without notice.
