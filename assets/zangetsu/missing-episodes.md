An episode list is only as fresh as the site the source reads.

1. **Update the source.** A catalogue that has not caught up is the most common reason.
2. Open the title from **another source** and compare. If the other source lists it, the title exists and this source's catalogue is behind.
3. Recent episodes often take a while to appear everywhere, and some sites list them late.
4. If the episode list shows a season you did not expect, check **Auto Resolve** and the title's metadata — a title can be split across seasons differently by different sites.

A title that no source lists at all is a catalogue gap in that ecosystem rather than a bug in the app.
