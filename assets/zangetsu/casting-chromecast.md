If you would rather not install anything on the TV, **cast from your phone to a Chromecast**.

Some streams need particular headers to play, which the app handles by **serving them through the phone**. So:

1. Keep the **phone awake** and on the **same network** as the Chromecast while casting.
2. If the cast stalls while the phone sleeps, that is why — the stream is being relayed through it.

Installing the app on an Android TV is the other option, and it gives you the full D-pad interface rather than a cast.
