Watching needs no sign-in, and progress syncs to your own account **if you sign in**, and to AniList/MAL/Simkl **if you connect them**.

**Incognito mode** pauses:
- watch history,
- external scrobbling,
- and Discord presence.

Turn it on when you do not want a session to count. The app itself has no ads and no analytics, so the history it keeps is for your own Continue Watching list.
