Watching needs **no sign-in**. A Zangetsu account is optional and only exists to sync your list across devices.

1. Open **Settings → Connections → Connect** and pick the tracker you want.
2. Sign in with that service and approve the connection.
3. Your watch and reading progress then sync to it, alongside your own Zangetsu list.

**On a TV**, use the QR flow instead of typing — see the TV sign-in entry.

AniList, MyAnimeList and Simkl are optional and independent of each other. Incognito mode pauses history and scrobbling while it is on.
