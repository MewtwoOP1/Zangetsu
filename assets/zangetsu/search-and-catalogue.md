- **Search** from the magnifying glass in the bar at the bottom.
- **Results are grouped by source**, so you can see at a glance who has the title.
- The **chip at the top of the home screen** switches which source you are browsing.
- Nothing matched? **Try fewer words**, and try the title as the source site lists it — original language, season number, and without punctuation.

A title only appears if a **source you have installed** has it. Each ecosystem has its own catalogue, so a title can be in the anime sources and missing from the movie ones, and a mode with no sources at all will find nothing.
