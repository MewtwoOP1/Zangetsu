Yes. The whole app is **open source under GPL-3.0**, and every release is built and published on the upstream repository, so anyone can read the code or build it themselves.

- **No ads, no analytics, and no account is needed to watch.**
- Android warns about installing outside the Play Store. That warning is about the install method, not about this app in particular.
- Download only from the official releases page: https://github.com/Spyou/Zangetsu/releases/latest

**On content, the project's own words are the plainest statement of what it is:**

> "Zangetsu is a tracking and management tool only — it does not host, provide, distribute, or maintain streaming content or extensions."

> "Users are solely responsible for how they use the app and any third-party services or extensions they choose to interact with."

> "The developer of Zangetsu disclaims all liability for misuse, legal issues, or violations arising from user actions. Legal concerns related to third-party services or extensions must be directed to their creators, not Zangetsu."

So: the app is a player and a tracker. The media comes from third-party services and extensions, which are their creators' responsibility, not the app's.
