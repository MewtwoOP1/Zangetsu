The app's project links three places:

- **Discord** — https://discord.gg/hey6vz9kg6
- **Reddit** — https://www.reddit.com/r/ZangetsuOfficial
- **Telegram** — https://t.me/ZangetsuStream

And the issue tracker, for anything that is really a bug: https://github.com/Spyou/Zangetsu/issues

The project's own issue template points blank issues at the guide, because **the guide answers most "how do I…" questions**. If you are about to open an issue about how to do something, the guide at https://zangetsu.online/guide/ is the faster route.

For a **vulnerability**, do not open a public issue — the repository has private vulnerability reporting enabled.
