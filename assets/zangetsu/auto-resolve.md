Auto Resolve is what **matches a catalogue entry to the right playable stream** when a title exists in more than one place, and it is what picks the **first server that responds** when you press play.

- If the stream it picks is poor, open the **sources sheet** in the player and pick another — your choice is remembered for that title.
- If it resolves to the **wrong title**, the source's metadata is wrong rather than the app. Check the title's page and compare with another source, and tell the source's author what it resolved to.
- A source that offers only one file per quality shows those as **separate servers** in the sources sheet, so what looks like a duplicate server may be a different quality.

See the entry on wrong episode or title for how to tell a metadata problem from a broken stream.
