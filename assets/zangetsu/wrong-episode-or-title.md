Two different things can cause this.

**The catalogue entry is wrong.** Different sites number seasons and episodes differently, and a title's metadata is taken from the source you opened it from.

1. Open the same title from **another source** and compare the episode list and description.
2. Check **Auto Resolve** — it is what matches a catalogue entry to the right stream, and it is worth re-running from the title's page.

**The stream is wrong.** The provider mapped the wrong file to the episode.

1. Open the **sources sheet** in the player and pick another server.
2. If every server of that source is wrong, it is the source's episode mapping, and the source's author needs to know.

Report it with the title, the episode number, the source name and the episode link, because that is what the author needs to fix it.
