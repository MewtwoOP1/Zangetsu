Zangetsu's native source type is the app's own **lightweight JavaScript** format. It installs and runs exactly like the app's own sources, and — unlike CloudStream and Aniyomi/Mihon extensions — it works on **iOS**, because it does not rely on Android's plugin loading.

Community-maintained repositories publish in this format too. They are not the app project's: they are run by other people, they can change or vanish at any time, and what they contain is not something the app project chooses. If one stops loading, that is the repository, not the app.
