Everything is built for a remote:

- **Arrow keys** to move.
- **OK** to select.
- **Back** to go up.
- **Holding OK** on a poster opens its quick menu, the same as a long-press on a phone.

The app detects the TV and switches to the D-pad interface automatically, so there is nothing to turn on.
