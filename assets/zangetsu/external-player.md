Yes — the player menu has an **external player** option, and it is also the fastest way to tell a player problem from a network problem.

It is also the documented next step when playback stalls or the picture is black: if the stream plays in the external player, the stream is fine and the app's own decoder is the problem.

The app's own player is **mpv** based, with speed control, subtitles and their styling, quality choices where the stream offers them, and gestures for seek, brightness and volume.
