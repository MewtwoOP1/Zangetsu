- Downloads live under the **Downloads** section of the app.
- You can **choose the folder** in settings. On a phone or tablet that is usually internal storage or an SD card; picking a removable card is a good way to keep big downloads off internal storage.
- If a download fails, check there is enough free space in the folder you chose.
- Removing a download from the Downloads section frees the space again.

Downloads are stored as ordinary video files, so a file manager can see them too.
