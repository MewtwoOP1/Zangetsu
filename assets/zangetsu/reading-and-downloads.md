There is a dedicated reader for **manga and light novels**, with its own sources and its own settings.

- **Reading and navigation** follow the source's own chapter list. Some manga are long vertical strips and some are paged; the reader follows what the source gives it.
- **Downloads** depend on the source supporting them, and they land in the same Downloads area as video downloads.
- To confirm a download works offline, put the device in airplane mode and open the chapter from the Downloads section.
- **Page loading** that is slow is usually the image host the source uses. Try another source for the same title before assuming the app is at fault.
- **Light novels** may be delivered as text chapters or as files; **EPUB** export is what turns a downloaded novel into a file you can read elsewhere.

Use the source switcher to move between sources while reading, exactly as you do while watching.
