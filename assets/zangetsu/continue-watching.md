Progress is saved automatically, so **Continue watching** on the home screen picks up where you stopped.

- Progress is kept on the device. If you signed in to a Zangetsu account, or connected AniList, MyAnimeList or Simkl, it also syncs there.
- **Incognito mode** pauses watch history, so a session in incognito does not appear in Continue Watching.
- If Continue Watching is empty, check that incognito is off and that you are on the profile you actually used.
